# steam-games

This app helps customers decide what games to buy by determining which games have the highest values based on their price.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Who your user is, what decision they have to make, and why it's hard without your app. (2-3 sentences)
My users are Steam gamers who are looking for a new game to play. They also are trying to get the most amount of enjoyment with the smallest amount of money. My app helps to aggregate all of the appropriate data and suggest choices based on my numerous calculations.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Play-Time** Which games give the most hours of play per dollar?
2. **Cost** Do higher costing games get better reviews?
3. **Active-Players** Which games have the largest active player communities relative to their price?

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
